<CaseStudySection id="context" title="A career transition creates work before the application" eyebrow="Context and ownership">

A job search splits across separate tools: research, resume, outreach, and tracking. I designed and engineered Offboard to connect that work, from product direction to operation. This story follows Job Packets, which turn a role into research and editable application materials.

</CaseStudySection>

<CaseStudySection id="system" title="Give each opportunity a persistent workspace" eyebrow="Product model">

I made the opportunity, not a tracker row, the organizing unit. It holds the role, company, people, research, materials, preparation, tasks, and history. LUMO, the AI layer, works across that context.

</CaseStudySection>

<CaseStudySection id="decision-context" title="Parse the role once, reuse it everywhere" eyebrow="Decision 03">

The workflow parses the role once and shares that research with resume work, outreach, and interview preparation. Reuse buys consistency at the risk of stale information.

</CaseStudySection>

<CaseStudySection id="decision-entry-point" title="A strong workflow can still be the wrong starting point" eyebrow="What changed">

A packet assumes the person already has a role worth pursuing. Someone new to a career transition may first need direction and a manageable next step. Early data, below, showed that gap.

So I made the start plan-first and moved packets later. The question became “Can this person take their first useful action?”

<CaseStudySection id="outcomes" title="Live, with early signal on where people start" eyebrow="Outcome">

Offboard is live. The numbers are early and small. They show use, not revenue, long-term retention, or the effect of the plan-first change.

- **The drop is the first packet, not onboarding.** Of 38 people who signed up in August 2026, 92% finished onboarding, 47% uploaded a resume, 45% ran a ghost check, and 8% built a packet in their first days.
- **The next problem is the second packet.** By September 2026, 72% of members with a full packet had built only one.
- **The ghost check spreads on its own.** One LinkedIn post drove 150 public ghost checks in 24 hours. The public checker ran about 500 analyses in its first five weeks.
- **Quality is measured.** In September 2026, job search put the right role in the top ten for all 20 test queries, up from one. LUMO passed 29 of 29 test cases, 22% faster, at about 80% lower model cost per run.
- **The system holds up.** The backend (188 functions, 175 tables) moved to a new Supabase project with about six minutes of downtime, no data lost, and no one signed out. It runs more than 200 server functions and about 4,500 automated tests.

</CaseStudySection>

<CaseStudySection id="learnings" title="Sequence is part of the product" eyebrow="Reflection">

A polished workflow can ask for the wrong commitment at the wrong time. Next, I would test the first journey with people at different stages of a transition, watch where they hesitate, and check that the next step is useful before measuring output.

</CaseStudySection>
